# Bill splitter

A page where a diner enters a bill's subtotal, tax, and tip percentage.

- **AC-1:** Entering a subtotal and tax shows the total (subtotal + tax).
- **AC-2:** The tip is calculated on the pre-tax subtotal. For a 100.00
  subtotal, 10.00 tax, and a 20% tip, the tip is 20.00 and the amount to pay
  is 130.00.
